## Problem

What is wrong, missing, or unclear today. Written so someone who was not in the
conversation can follow it.

## Goal

What is true once this is done. Concrete enough to tell whether it happened.

## Non-goals

What this deliberately does not cover. This is the section that keeps a spec
from quietly growing.

## Approach

How it will work. Enough detail to disagree with before the code exists.

## Verification

How we will know it works, in terms of the gate in Article 3. Which checks
cover it, and what a human still has to look at.

## Open questions

Anything undecided. A spec can be accepted with open questions; it cannot be
marked `done` with them.
